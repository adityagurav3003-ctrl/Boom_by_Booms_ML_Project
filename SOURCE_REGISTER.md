# Source register for final dashboard additions

## 1. Space-industry imports and exports

Department of Space / ISRO, Lok Sabha Budget Session 2023 parliamentary answer. The answer states that year-wise data on exports and imports carried out in the space technology industry since 2014 are given in ₹ crore, covering FY2014-15 through FY2021-22.

Primary source:
https://www.isro.gov.in/media_isro/pdf/docs/UsefulLinks/ParliamentQuestions/Budget_Session_2023.pdf

## 2. GDP

MoSPI National Accounts data are the macroeconomic source family. For the dashboard's ratio calculations, the 2011-12-base historical vintage used in the 2024-25 Economic Survey is retained consistently for FY2014-15–FY2024-25, with current-price and constant-price GDP.

MoSPI catalogue:
https://esankhyiki.mospi.gov.in/catalogue-main/catalogue?page=0&product=NAS&rowsPerPage=10

2024-25 Economic Survey source used for FY2023-24 and FY2024-25 values:
https://himachalservices.nic.in/economics/en-IN/eco-survey-2024-25.html

## 3. INR/USD exchange rate

RBI annual-average exchange-rate table is used through FY2023-24. FY2024-25 annual average is ₹84.57/US$, consistent with RBI/FBIL-referenced reporting.

RBI historical table:
https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/PublicationsView.aspx?id=22613

## 4. Defence exports

Defence Development / Ministry of Defence official series already incorporated into the project dataset.

## 5. NSIL financial series

NSIL annual-report series already incorporated into the project dataset and used only for the company-level space forecast/context graph.

## 6. Public-private space collaboration events

ISRO / Department of Space / IN-SPACe and Technology Development Board official documentation already incorporated into the project context layer.
